AI / Semis
Investment thesis
Nvidia's CUDA ecosystem creates significant switching costs — a classic barrier to entry from microeconomics. As AI model training scales, demand for GPU compute is inelastic in the short run, giving Nvidia real pricing power. I'm watching whether this economic moat holds as AMD and custom silicon (Google TPU, Apple) start to compete meaningfully.

MSFT

35%
Cloud / AI
Investment thesis
Azure's cloud growth is tied to enterprise AI adoption, which I expect to follow an S-curve diffusion pattern. Microsoft's OpenAI partnership gives it a distribution advantage — it embeds AI into products already with near-monopoly enterprise penetration. The network effects here are structural and hard to replicate quickly.
