# AhaanEconomusings

A personal platform tracking real investment decisions and economics commentary, built by Ahaan Jain — a Grade 11 IB student at North London Collegiate School, Dubai.

## What this is

Two things in one site:

- **Portfolio** — live holdings from an IBKR account, each with a written investment thesis grounded in economic reasoning (switching costs, network effects, S-curve adoption, discount rates)
- **Musings** — original economics posts written for a general audience, connecting IB Economics HL concepts to real market events, UAE macro, and global finance

## Why it exists

Most people who follow markets consume content. This site is about producing it — documenting the reasoning behind each investment decision and explaining economic ideas in plain language. The goal is a body of work that compounds over time, not a one-off entry.

## Stack

Single-file static site — HTML, CSS, vanilla JS. No frameworks, no dependencies, no build step. Hosted on Netlify.
